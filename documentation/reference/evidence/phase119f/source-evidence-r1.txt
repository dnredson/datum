Claim: Operational-reconciliation authorization persistence is keyed by exactly `(project_id,
node_id)` — no revision, service, or operation component — and issuing a new durable authorization
for that key structurally replaces (overwrites) whatever authorization previously occupied it.
DServer does not retain a durable history of prior immutable authorization snapshots for a node.
